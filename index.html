📍 Geo Mapper

Create custom maps from CSV data

How to use:

  • Upload a CSV file with georeferenced data (latitude and longitude in separate columns)
  • Configure the visualisation using drop-down menus in the left panel (toggle open/close by clicking the eye icon 👁️)
  • Choose from a selection of basemaps and overlays
  • Customize data visualisation including proportional symbols, colour selection
  • Customise pop-ups including numerical and categorical data, images and charts
  • Measurement and drawing tools
  • View the Legend in the right panel (toggle open/close by clicking the key icon 🗝️)
  • Save your work using export (html and GeoJSON files) and share options
