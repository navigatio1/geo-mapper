About this data

Mean PISA mathematics scores for 15-year-olds. 79 of the 81 PISA 2022 participants are available to choose from. 25 of those have the full eight-cycle history back to 2000; the other 54 currently show only their verified 2022 score, appearing as a single point rather than a full trajectory (see "Coverage" below) — historical data for those can be added over time.

Coverage and exclusions

Two of the 81 published PISA 2022 rows are left out entirely: "Baku (Azerbaijan)" and "Ukrainian regions (18 of 27)" are partial/subnational samples, not full-country results — the same reason China (Shanghai-only in 2009/2012, a wider but still partial sample later) is excluded. Singapore has no data before 2009 (it didn't participate yet); its line starts there rather than being drawn back to zero. The United Kingdom's figures are only OECD-comparable from 2006 onward — its 2000 and 2003 rounds both had response-rate problems and are excluded rather than shown as unreliable numbers.

About the flags

Flags are drawn as plain SVG shapes — colour bands, crosses, circles — not emoji or image files, so they render identically on every device rather than depending on a font that may or may not include them. Most are simplified: correct colours and basic layout, but stars, emblems, crests and fine detail are generally omitted. A handful of PISA participants aren't standard sovereign ISO 3166-1 entries (Chinese Taipei, Kosovo, the Palestinian Authority); their codes follow the closest widely-used convention rather than a formal ISO code.

Why "GBR"?

Every code on this chart (FIN, JPN, GBR, and so on) is the ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code — the same three-letter country codes used by the UN, ISO, and most passport and banking systems worldwide. This is not a choice made by this app: under that international standard, the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland is simply coded GBR, the same way Germany is DEU and Switzerland is CHE. See the separate note below on what the UK's underlying PISA figure actually represents.

A note on "United Kingdom"

The UK figure is the OECD's single combined result for England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland weighted together — not England alone. The four nations diverge meaningfully: in PISA 2022, England's maths score was well above Wales's, with Scotland and Northern Ireland in between. Treat "United Kingdom" here as a UK-wide average, not a stand-in for any one nation.
